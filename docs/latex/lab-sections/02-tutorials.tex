\section{Guided tutorials}

The \textbf{Tutorials \& Documentation} button in the header opens a
picker of short, self-contained modules, plus a \textbf{Documentation}
section linking the same two PDF manuals this crate ships everywhere else
--- the CLI manual and this one. Each module covers one part of the lab;
picking one is not a commitment to the others, and they can be taken in
any order.

\subsection{How a step works}

Each step spotlights the part of the page it is about and shows a tooltip
with an explanation. A step comes in one of two kinds:

\begin{itemize}
  \item An \textbf{informational} step just explains something; a
  \textbf{Next} button advances it.
  \item A \textbf{gated} step (marked ``Waiting for you to try it\ldots'')
  names a real action to perform --- insert a drive, open a file, switch
  identity, send a letter --- and does not advance until that action
  actually happens in the lab. Completion is checked against the lab's own
  state (the current snapshot, or the most recent entry in the Activity
  log), not against a click on the tooltip itself, so there is no way to
  fake having done it. Once satisfied, the step marks itself done and
  advances on its own a moment later. \textbf{Skip this step} is always
  available if you would rather move on without doing it.
\end{itemize}

A step can also relocate its target as the page changes under it --- for
example, tracking a folder tile in the File Explorer until it is opened,
then tracking the file row that takes its place --- so one step can walk
you through a short sequence rather than only ever pointing at one fixed
element.

\subsection{The modules}

The picker groups its thirteen modules into three categories; taking one
module is never a commitment to the others in its category, or to the
category itself.

\subsubsection{Identities \& drives}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Identities \& drives} --- who you're acting as, bringing a
  mock USB drive online, reading the organization key tree's own slice, and
  switching identity.
  \item \textbf{Move \& copy slots} --- move and copy slots, then inspect
  how containers affect physical-device policy.
  \item \textbf{Provision \& register a leaf} --- provision a fresh device
  slot, then register it in the organization tree.
  \item \textbf{Key administration} --- revoke, reissue, restructure,
  countersign, and understand parent approval.
  \item \textbf{Bridges \& remote devices} --- manage cross-branch
  visibility and distinguish local device actions from relay transfer.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Files \& unlocking}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Files \& unlocking} --- opening a public file, then trying
  a quorum-protected one to see what the access trace looks like when a
  single identity does not meet its threshold.
  \item \textbf{Complete a quorum unlock} --- read a file's requirements,
  connect cooperating devices, and complete an unlock.
  \item \textbf{Passwords \& PINs} --- create and unlock the lab's second,
  independent protection scheme.
  \item \textbf{Export bundles \& share links} --- contrast
  recipient-sealed export bundles with bearer share links.
  \item \textbf{Signatures, properties \& expiry} --- sign and verify
  through a bridge, inspect properties, and observe destructive expiry.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Mailbox: sending \& receiving}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Mailbox: sending \& receiving} --- sealing a file to
  another label, switching to that identity, and receiving it from the
  inbox.
  \item \textbf{Reject \& acknowledge} --- exercise the rejection path and
  explicitly refresh a sender's acknowledgement state.
  \item \textbf{Updates \& device letters} --- see which higher-level
  workflows use sealed letters and where their controls actually live.
\end{itemize}

Finishing a module's last step opens a short completion notice; from
there, or from \textbf{Exit tutorial} at any point, the \textbf{Tutorials
\& Documentation} button reopens the picker for another module.
